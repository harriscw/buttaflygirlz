QUIZ: ARE YOU A PERFECTIONIST?

Your room is usually:

A. Mostly clean with a bit of clutter

B. Spotless

C. A disaster area!

How long does it take you to edit your English essay:

A. 30 to 60 minutes

B. 4 hours

C. 2 minutes max.

You check your makeup:

A. About twice a day

B.  3 times per class

C.  Never

Others describe you as :

A. Pretty neat

B. Flawless

C. Sloppy

After lunch, you:

A. Pop a piece of gum

B. Brush and floss your teeth

C. Just go to class

Scoring:

Mostly A's: You are pretty normal. You're not extreme so your habits don't freak people out. You care about your appearance but not so much that when people bump into in the hall, they're not scared for their life. You're keeping it real!

Mostly B's: Cool it, chica! You need to calm down and realize that no one's perfect and that includes you! Don't always try so hard cuz chances are you probably look fine anyways. Also a perfectionist is intimidating to others because they're afraid that they live up to your high standards.

Mostly C's: You're a slacker! It's cool that you're not totally into your looks but not caring about your appearance at all isn't that great either. You need to find a balance in between the two. Also messy people are sometimes seen as not caring enough about something to do it right!

I have like really dry flaky skin on my face and have no idea what to use! I've tried everything. Even something a doctor said would be a good thing to try (I was in to get a physical and he said it would be good to try) It dries out my skin even when I wash my face but, of course, I have to wash my face or else I'll have a zit prob!!! any suggestions?

Dear Makeup Lady,
I get enough sleep most of the time and I'm not allergic to anything, but I still have dark circles under my eyes! What could I do to prevent them or get rid of them all together?
     - Anon

Dear Anon,
You might want to try putting herbal tea bags on your eyelids. First, soak the teabag for about 30 seconds, then squeeze out the water and place them on top of your closed eyes for 5-10 minutes. This relieves dark coloration and puffiness. FYI: Chamomile is the best herbal bag for your eyes.Your dark circles may have been genetically inherited, so it may be impossible to completely get rid of them. To hide your dark circles, I recommend Physician's Formula Gentle Cover Concealer in Yellow. It is formulated to hide dark circles!
    -The Makeup Lady

Dear Al and Val,
Hello. My friend at my school is always in style. She buys her friends OPI nail polish and she takes them to salons and stuff. We were friends but then she hated me for not being in style. My mom buys me tight not loose fitting clothes. I think I am totally clueless. My nails are bare and lack color. My hair is messed up and my face is clear. Please help!!!
        - Lost

Dear Lost,
First, a true friends wouldn't care if you were stylish or not. A real friend would like you for who you are. But if you want to know how to become in-style, you first have to decide what kind of style you like. We think having your own unique is way kewler than following the trends. If you want to follow the trends though, look at magazines such as Seventeen and Teen, or at shops like Wet Seal and Macy's. Some interesting easy ways to wear your hair include up in a hair-claw, straight down, or up in a loose chignon bun. There is always the classic ponytail too. On your nails, try pastel or glitter colors cuz they seem to be trendy this season!
         -Al and Val

Dear Al and Val,
I've been going out with this guy for about 4 months. He's in 7th grade & I'm in 8th. But now I like someone else. This someone is in my grade and is a lot cuter and I have more in common with him. What should I do? If I dump the guy I'm going with now, he'll hate me and be very upset.
        - Anonymous

Dear Anonymous,
You're young, your current boyfriend probably didn't expect your relationship to last forever. You have to explain to him that you like someone else. Tell him that you still like him and want to be his friend. Make sure to stay in contact with him otherwise your hope of friendship with him will diminish. Explain your reasons for breaking up with him. We know how hard it is to dump someone but you shouldn't be unhappy when you could be happy with this new boy. Let your current boy down easy.
        -Al and Val

It takes two minutes to dry, but the extra thirty seconds are worth waiting for. Don't put it on too thick though cuz then it's gloppy and peels off easily. Great Color: Brassy

SPORTZ: SOCCER BY Pecan2417

Soccer is not a sport with just running or speed. You have to have a lot of skills to play good, and it takes a lot of practice. I have been playing for about 12 seasons. I practice with the team twice a week for 2 hours, and I practice about 1 hour by myself or with a friend on the days that there are no practices. The practices are really hard. We have to walk for 5 minutes, then jog for 10, then sprint for 5, and then walk for 10. That may not sound hard, but, when you get out there and try it, you'll understand. Then we work on drills. We have to head the balls, and the balls are normally really hard (don't think of that in a nasty way), we have to practice throw-in's, and dribbling. After that, we have about 45 minutes to scrimmage as well as we can. But, all the practice pays off on game days, if you work hard for what you want and practice, you can get it in no time.
