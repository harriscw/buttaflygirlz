SHYNESS: HOW TO DEAL FROM A SHY GAL'S POINT OF VIEW BY Val

So, you know that feeling shy girls. You've got all the right things to say at all the right times, but something is holding you back. You walk by your crush and all you can do is lower your head and hope he doesn't see your bright red face. Yep, I think we have all had that experience. I definitely have. I can't really blame my shyness on other people. I would really like to blame it on my parents for having only one child but I know that is truly not their fault. I wish I had a magic pill that I could take and all my worries of what to say and when to talk would vanish. But since that isn't invented yet, I'll just have to deal with working it out with myself.

You can definitely say that the key to breaking your shyness is self-confidence. So how do you get that? When I think of self-confidence, I think, "you know I'm not all that bad." I wish I could walk down the street without wondering why that person is looking at me or if they care that I am having a bad hair day. Self-confidence would be telling someone how you feel without worrying what they think about it. If I had more self-confidence I would be able to go up to a guy and express my feelings about him to his face. Shyness is one of the most useless stupidest emotions. If you believe in yourself and think that you are no less of a person than others, you can jump over this emotion and then see what life can offer you, without it! I am still trying to jump over this hurdle of life.

Here's a tip: Wet your eyelashes before applying mascara to keep them clump-free

Dear Makeup Lady,
I have really fair skin and a lot of freckles and when I try to put base on it looks all heavy and weird. What can I do to fix this?
        -Anonymous

Dear Anonymous,
Ok, so I suggest that you try Cover Girl's Simply Powder. It's lighter than foundation and it feels like a powder but it has more coverage. Another suggestion is translucent pressed powder in fair or ivory. If you get your basic skin tone, it matches virtually anyone. It's very versatile. A third suggestion is to add Bonne Bell No Shine to your foundation. It makes it sheerer and lighter. If you don't want to buy anymore makeup, then I suggest you use the foundation only on your problem areas and blend well. And DON'T TRY TO HIDE YOUR FRECKLES! They are cute and they give you character.
        -The Makeup Lady

FLIPS AND TIPS

Hey all you thin-haired people! Tired of limp locks with little luster? Try Back to Basics chamomile volumizing spray before you blow dry. Spray it onto your roots for added lift. Add a finishing shine onto your hair with Kava's Sunshine spray right before you walk out the door.

Are you locks dry, full of tangles and break easy? It could be your shampoo and conditioner. A good shampoo to try is Iso Reviving Shampoo, which moisturizes and balances your hair. Follow it up with Kenra Re-moisturizer conditioner and in no time you'll have healthy beautiful hair.

For those who missed it, OPI nail color has some fabulous colors in their Fall/Winter line. Some favs included Los Angeles Latte, Chicago Champagne Frost, and Tucunari Boysenberry. These polishes are awesomely fast drying and relatively chip free.

Do you bite your nails? Well, here's the solution you've been waiting for. Perfect Nail came out with a bottle of Don't Bite It. Put it on like a nail polish, it's salon tested formula contains a very bitter but harmless ingredient which will discourage even the most avid nail nibbler.
