Dear Skin Trouble,
Instead of trying to worry about what type of foundation to use, you should combat the problem from the start. Take care of that dry skin! Use a moisturizing lotion after you've been out in the sun. Then experiment with foundations and powders to see which works better with your skin.
    - The Makeup Lady

Score a Perfect "Tan"

First, the most important thing to realize is that if you expose yourself to the skin too much, you can develop skin cancer which can be deadly. This doesn't mean that you still can't get a nice tan, you just have to be careful. The following are some tips to attain a nice, safe tan!

Always wear some sunscreen (It's best to wear at least SPF 15, but even SPF 4 is better than nothing.)

Build up your tan! Don't just go out and expect to get a tan in one day. It's much better to go out little by little and get your tan in "layers" than to go all at once! Besides, a "built-up" tan lasts much longer than a one-day version!

If you do get sunburned, take care of your skin! Use lotions and Aloe Vera gels to soothe the pain and redness.

The safest way to get a tan is through a bottle! Sporting a good "fake" tan is not only safer but also can provide a more golden tan for people that don't tan that way naturally! The most important thing in using "bottled-sun" is that you test it on a small patch of skin in a place where people won't see it first. This is so you make sure you're not allergic to it and that the color looks natural on you!

FLIPS AND TIPS

Well girlies, toenail painting season has started and I just know you want tips on how to enhance your digits. Well, a great way to start a pedicure is to soak your feet in warm sudsy water. This cleans away any gunk on your feet! Then clip your nails to the desired length. First put a layer of base coat on your toes. This will protect the nail and make the paint apply better. Then apply two coats of your favorite color! We recommend the fast drying stuff because we don't like to sit around! Then finish 'em up with a shiny top coat to prevent chipping. If you'll be painting your toenails a lot this summer, then you might want to invest in a toe-separator. They are made out of foam and they keep you toes apart so you don't smudge the paint. They are really very useful.

You are probably thinking, now I know how to paint my nails, what colors should I use? This season there is a wide variety of choose from. We recommend investing in some sheer polishes. Not only are they super-cool but they look pretty natural as well. Maybelline Express Finish has about eight different colors to choose from and is a steal at three buck a bottle. Extra bonus: They dry fast! Other choices include beach-like colors, like sand, peach, and yellow. These too are natural looking. Cover Girl's Seashell Story line has some great colors.

We understand how harsh the sun and salt water can be on your hair so we did some research on how to protect it. The easiest and cheapest solution is to wear a hat. Not only does it protect your hair from the sun's heat  but it covers your face too so you don't burn. If you're too afraid of hat-hair, we recommend Bumble and Bumble's Sun Spray. It also has aloe-vera gel in it which will cool your scalp down. Price setback: about 10 bucks. Also look on the labels of your favorite shampoos. Some shampoos and conditioners actually contain UV protectors.

Dear Sam:
What kind of exercise can I do to get rid of that flabby skin under my arms?

Dear Reader:
Well, there are a couple of things you can do. A) you can have a liposuction (I wouldn't recommend it though) or B) you can work it away! Here a two exercises that will work away that fat. (I'm not promising that it will fly off!)

1. The Booty bump ~~ Yup! You heard it! This one is gonna be hard to describe. Sit on the floor with your legs straight out in front of you. Put your hands on the floor by your butt and point your fingers towards your toes. Then bend your knees and lift your butt off the ground using your arms. This is kinda a reverse push-up. Repeat as many times you can (that is, until your arms feel like they are going to fall off) and them rest. Once you feel better, repeat AMTAP (as many times as possible) again. This tightens the triceps.

2. Push-ups ~~ I know that you guys know what these are, but MOST people do them wrong. I'll give you the easy ones, most sexists call them Girl Push-Ups! Kneel down on all fours. Don't let your feet touch the ground. Then slowly bend your arms until your stomach is on the ground. Slowly straighten them and you have completed one "Girl Push-up." Do roughly 20.~Sam

Two-piece suits that flatter your bod by Al and Val

If you're small chested stay away from halter-like swimsuits. They will de-emphasize your top half. Instead try a suit with a bra-style top which will add some curves to your upper bod!

If you're big-busted, try one of the new sports-bra like tops with skinny straps. Not only are they super-stylish but they will give you support too!

If you've got a bit of a tummy, look for suits where the top has some detailing like ruffles, a stripe of another color, etc. This will draw attention away from your stomach and focus it on your top half!

If you've got a butt, then try some boy-cut bottoms. Depending on how they're cut they can either make your butt look smaller or blow it up! Ask for a bud's honest opinion!

The most important swim-suit tip is that you like the suit! If you only halfway like something, don't convince yourself into buying it, you'll never wear it!
