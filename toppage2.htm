Empire Records (Butta-Fly)
This movie is so funny! It stars Joe LaPaglia, Johnny Whitworth(a major hottie), Ethan Embry, Liv Tyler and Renee Zellweger(from Jerry Maguire). It takes place at this record store in a little town and it is all about the completely different people that work there. It chronicles a day at the store (Empire Records) and tons of weird stuff happens! It has so hilarious dialogue and the soundtrack is great too!

Misery (Butta-Fly)
This is one of my favorite scary movies! It stars James Cahn and Kathy Bates(who won an Academy Award for this movie). James Cahn plays a famous novelist who gets in a car crash and is saved by his "number one fan" played by Kathy Bates. This movie is incredibly creepy and Kathy is a physchoic fan. It is a must see for any horror movie lover!

A Fish Called Wanda (Butta-Fly)
This movie is my all time fav! It is so hilarious. It stars a lot of people from the cast of the British comedy show Monty Python. Also starring are Jamie Lee Curtis and Kevin Kline (who won an award for this movie) I laughed harder than I have ever laughed before at this movie! It is sick humor but I love it. The movie takes place after a bank heist and all the characters interact with each other in such twisted ways that you can't help but laugh. It is hard to explain the movie but I definately reccommend renting it today!

The Shining (Butta-Fly)
This movie is the best horror movie!! It freaked me out so bad the first time I saw it. It stars Jack Nicholson and Shelly Duvall as a married couple who decide to be winter caretakers for this huge resort in the mountains. Nicholson know's about the hotel's horrible past but doesn't tell his wife about it because he doesn't want to scare her. When they arrive with their son, you find out about his special power of seeing the future. Well anyways, Nicholson experiences cabin fever while he's up there and starts to go physcho! That's when it gets really scary! You shouldn't watch this movie alone!

Bye-Bye Love (Super Fly)
I don't know what it is about this movie but I can watch it over and over again without getting sick of it. It's not even my favorite movie but it's just one I like a lot! This flic stars Paul Riser, Janeane Garafaolo, Randy Quaid and Matthew Modine. It is about three divorced dad's and the pain of dealing with their wives when they take care of the kids. Johnny Whitworth also plays a small part in this movie. It's a really cute movie. My favorite scence is when Quaid goes out on a date with Garafaolo. It is so funny because she is playing this amazingly synical bitter women who can't make up her mind about anything.

Pulp Fiction (Butta-Fly)
This movie is extremely vulgar and grusome but something about it is so appealing. The characters are disgusting and they are really scummy but that is half the fun. Pulp Fiction stars Samuel L. Jackson, John Travolta, Uma Thurman, Harvey Keitel, and Bruce Willis. It is about two hitmen played by Jackson and Travolta who get into strange situations. There's not much dialogue left after you remove the swear words but what's left is really clever. My favorite part is the whole thing with Travolta's date with Uma. If you are bothered by vulgar language and violence, you shouldn't see this movie!
