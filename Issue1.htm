THE MAKEUP COUNTER

The new makeup looks for winter and fall involve a lot of brown, gold, copper, and moss colors. The whole look is very bronzy and matte. Although frost are still kewl as long as they are subdued. If you can only buy one product this season, buy Bronzing Powder. My fav is the cheap Bonne Bell Bronzing Powder. It's great and not expensive. You need to use a big blusher brush to put on the apples of your cheeks and dust lightly everywhere else. You could also use it as an eye shadow and even a primer for your lips. A good tip for making your lips looks fuller is to use a matte lipstick and then dab a frost or gloss in the middle of your lower lip. Then smack your lips together and VOILA! Fuller lips in ten seconds.
