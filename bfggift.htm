BFG HOLIDAY GIFT GUIDE

Whether you celebrate Kwanzaa, Christmas, Hanukah, or whatever holiday shopping can be tough. You want to find that perfect gift at a reasonable price but you have no clue where to begin. That's why we made "The BFG Gift Guide." It's a great resource for different price ranges and people. There are 3 different price ranges (with really cheesy titles!)- Cheap Skate ($5 and under), Average Joe ($6-15), and High Roller ($16 and up). There are also three different people categories- Buds, Relatives, and Significant Others. We've even got a special section for online friends and school/work aquaintances (People who you're friends with but you don't usually hang out with.) Plus for each gift we've got cool wrapping hints!

Cheap Skate ($5 and under)

Buds

Make a small "goodie bag." Look at party stores for fun "knick-knacks" like humorous buttons, patches, etc. Also add candy, and whatever else you want.
Wrapping Tip: Ask for a couple extra takeout boxes at your fav Chinese food place. Put tissue paper inside and fill with goodies. They might charge you a little bit but it's still a cool, cheap, toteable package.

Make homemade clips! Remember those awesome colored bobby pins from Delia*s a while back that were super-cool but pretty expensive? Well they're way easy to make yourself. Buy a package or two of plain bobby pins (about $1 for 70). Haul out all your old nail polish and paint away. If you run out or can't part with your polish, pick up a couple bottle from Wet 'n' Wild ($1 a pop)
Wrapping Tip: Put them in cute little zip up pencil-pouch sized bags. Sometimes they have them for really cheap at places like Target and Mervyns.

Give your bud a gift certificate for a movie party. Tell 'em you'll let them rent any movie they want and you'll watch it and feast on popcorn and other movie snacks.
Wrapping Tip: Put it in a box and wrap it in homemade movie collage wrap. Cut out pics from your old mags and paste them on either plain white paper or the backside of other wrapping paper.

Relatives

A couple years ago I created a "cookbook" of my favorite recips for my relatives. They don't have to be your own recips as long as you're not selling the book. Just type 'em up. Add some cool fonts or clp art adn print. Voila! A cheap, unique gift that's re-printable for all your relatives.
Wrapping Tip: Tie a wooden cooking spoon on with a ribbon for a cooking flair.

Bake a loaf of your scrumptious banana bread or a batch of your super chocolate chip cookies. Whatever's your speciality. Buy a cheap plastic or glass dish (you can use paper too but it's flimsy.)
Wrap Tip: Use colored/printed cellophane wrap. It keeps food fresh and look pretty too!

Make some pretty pressed flower bookmarks or suncatchers! Find some cool leaves or flowers (if none are left at home, buy a cheap boquet) Haul out your super heavy books (dictionaries and encyclopedias work best) Cut off a flower/leaf you like. Now take a piece of waxed paper that's approxiamently twice as big as your flower and fold it in half. Put the flower/leaf inside and slide it inside the middle of a heavy book. Repeat with as many flowers as needed. Stack more books on top. Leave for 1-2 weeks. Arrange dried flowers and put between two sheets of clear contact paper. Cut out in rectangles for bookmarks and irregular shapes for suncatchers. Add a string and maybe a hook for suncatchers.
Wrap Tip: For bookmarks, slip inside a book. For suncatchers, print up instructions to hang in a sunny place. Make it cute by writing the instructions in poem form.

Significant Other

Make a memory collage. Find all those movie stubs from flicks you saw together (if you can't find 'em try nd look for pictures from the movie.) Also add in pics of the two of you together. You can get cutesy and add hearts and "I Love You" stuff too but don't go overboard, it will look cheesy.
Wrap Tip: Just leave it the way it is. It's beautiful!

Make a spaghetti dinner and watch Lady and the Tramp. It's super romantic in that cute lil' kid kind of way. Pasta's cheap and you can make your own sauce or buy a can. It'll probably cost around the same price. Light some candles for ambience.
Wrapping Tip: Make a gift certificate. It'll make it look like you spent tons of time planning it.

Make a mixed tape of "your songs." Also add songs with hidden messages and hidden jokes. You'll make your love smile, laugh , maybe even cry from your personalized tape.
Wrapping Tip: Make the cassette cover cool! Decorate with pics or use a cool font. Personalize it and give it a good title.

Average Joe ($6-$15)

Buds

Give a gift subscription to a magazine. Lots of magazines have super-cheap deals for gift subscriptions. I just got a renewal notice from Teen where when I renewed, I got a free! gift subscription for a friend. Last time I checked Spin (music magazine), Teen, Jump, Twist, Mademoiselle, and Jane were all under $15 to subscribe. It's a gift that lasts the whole year long!
Wrapping Tip: Make a cool card/certificate telling them about their present.

Watch for sales at your favorite stores like Garden Botanika. They're always having big blowouts especially during the holidays. They usually have bath kits with lotion, shampoo/conditioner, soap, body spray, etc. on sale for around $15 during the holidays. Also sometimes they have sales on their fabulous makeup (like 6 for $25). Pick some up for a friend and get some for yourself too.
Wrapping Tip: For irregularlly shaped gifts, bags are the best! Find some cheap bags at stores like Target and Wallmart!

Buy your bud a copy of their favorite flick! Video stores usually have big sales during the holidays too. Some good ideas include: Empire Records, Ferris Bueller's Day Off, Pretty in Pink, Good Will Hunting.
Wrapping Tip: Wrap with the special movie box things they sell at the store. They fit perfectly!

Relatives

Make a calandar. Find 12 pics you like and get them blown up (if you want them bigger.) You could also scan them into your computer if you have a scanner. Make the calendar on your computer (I use Microsoft Publisher but most print shop programs have this option). Add family b-days, anniversaries, etc. Once you've got all twelve months together, get it bound at the copy shop (usually costs between $3-5). Voila! Your own homeade calendar.
Wrapping Tip: Use the funnies (comics) section of the paper.

Buy a cheap genealogy computer program. Some versions are super low priced (even under $15) for that history buff. Genealogy is actually the most popular hobby for people over 40! Try a discount computer store like CompUSA.
Wrap Tip: Use an old map. Or get a new one at AAA.

Buy them a book about their favorite hobby. Search Amazon.com for unusual ones. They'll enjoy the fact that you remember their fav pastime.
Wrap Tip: Brown paper sponged with holiday colors.

Significant Other

Movie gift certificates. This is perfect for next time you wanna go out but don't have cash. Some theaters even offer free food coupons when you purchase movie certificates.
Wrapping Tip: Use an old video box, then wrap in gift paper.

Get 'em that CD they've had their eye on. CDs seem generic but they are a useful gift and are appreciated. Just make sure that the receiver will enjoy the CD before you get it. They might feel obligated to keep the gift even if they don't like it.
Wrapping Tip: Wrap in a copy of sheet music to stay with the music theme.

Buy a cool poster to hang on the wall. Everytime your special someone looks at it they'll be reminded of you:)
Wrapping Tip: Since posters are odd shaped, wrap in tissue paper and tie ribbons on the loose ends so it looks kinda like a tootsie roll.

High Roller ($16 and up)

Buds

Give your physic buddy a special outing day. Go to a palm reader or a tarot card reader. After you get your fortunes read by a professional, go out to lunch at a Chinese place for "fotune cookies." Then end your day watching a scary movie like Carrie (cuz she's telekinetic).
Wrapping tip: Put a bow around your wrist and say your theirs for the day.

Get all dolled up and have a glamour night. Treat your friend to dinner (it doesn't have to be super fancy, its just fun going out all made up.) Go the whole nine yards, the more glamourized you look, the more fun it is.
Wrapping Tip: Give you bud a disposeable camera to capture the glamour escapade.

Buy your fav shopping bud a copy of Fashion Trip! It's a totally cool new program where you can try on hip clothes from companies like Bongo, Levis, Steve Madden, Girl Star and tons of others. You pick a body type that is like yours and it simulate how you'd look in the clothes. If you like what you see, then you can really buy it! You and a friend can go online and use virtual dressing rooms to get their opinion. Plus there are fashion tips from Seventeen!
Wrapping Tip: Use a bag from your fav store with tissue paper!

Relatives

Give that favorite relative a gift certificate for a pedicure. Many older people get manicures but they never indulge in a pedicure. Most places massage your feet a little, and polish your toes. It's actually really cool feeling.
Wrapping Tip: Attach a small bottle of polish with the certificate.

Give 'em gift certificates to their fav restaurant! Hey maybe they'll even take you out!
Wrapping Tip: Wrap with a cooking utensil attached (like a wooden spoon or wire whisk)

Buy them a photo enhancing kit to spice up those family reunion pics! Try Kai's Power Goo it's awesome!
Wrap Tip: Attach a fun picture!

Significant Other

Treat them to a night out! Buy the movie tickets, dinner and dessert!
Wrapping Tip: Tie a ribbon around yourself:)

Get tickets to their favorite sporting event and enjoy the game! (or at least pretend you like it!)
Wrap Tip: Use the sports section of the newspaper as wrapping paper!

Pre-order tickets to an upcoming concert! It'll be a blast!
Wrap Tip: Use those CD boxes at the Wherehouse!
