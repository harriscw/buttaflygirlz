QUIZ: HOW DO YOU HANDLE YOUR MONEY?

On a regular shopping trip, you typically spend:

A. All of your money plus some of your buds

B. Most of your money

C.  About $5

You just got invited to a "cool" party, to splurge you buy:

A. A new outfit including shoes, and accessories

B. A new shirt or skirt

C. A pack of gum

You usually buy your clothes from the ___ rack:

A. One rack, you shop them all!

B. Sale

C. Clearance

For lunch break from shopping, you eat at the:

A.Pricey Italian place

B. Mid-Price burger joint

C. Cheapest place, usually T-Bell

When you get cash for your birthday, you:

A. Blow it all the next day

B. Spend part of it, and save the rest

C. Save every cent

Scoring:

Mostly A's:  You are a high-spending chica! Yes, buying loads of stuff is fun now, but you'll be bummed for buying so much, when you really need the cash later! Start saving, even if it's just a little bit at a time. Money adds up over time.

Mostly B's: You have a pretty good balance between spending and saving. You don't deprive yourself of occasional splurges but you know that the money may mean more later.

Mostly C's: You're a penny pincher! It's great to save money! You won't regret it when you're off at college! It is okay to splurge every once and a while though!

Dear Makeup Lady,
Ok, I have BIG puffy, dark circle under my eyes, I'm on a tight budget, and I can't use all that foundation, concealer things topped on. Actually, I can't use foundation all together. What should I do? Thanks.
    -Michelle

Dear Michelle,
For an easy fix without makeup,  here are a couple things you can do to help eliminate your dark circles and puffiness. Try putting a soaked tea bag on each eye. Chamomile is the best flavor but any one will do. Leave them there for 15 minutes. Another thing to try, is a cool beauty mask. Put a gel  beauty mask in the refrigerator for one hour, then place it on your face for 20-30 minutes. The simplest solution is to sleep! Puffy eyes are usually a sign that you are not getting enough sleep.
    - The Makeup Lady

Dear Makeup Lady,
I have a hair question....Is there any way to highlight my hair without using stuff like Sun-In or bleach? Does lemon juice really work? Thanx! :)
   -High-lightened

Dear High-lightened,
Yes, lemon juice does work! But it's basically the same thing as Sun-In. You just put a little bit of lemon juice where you want your highlights and then go out in the sun! With Sun-In, the lightened parts come faster with the aid of a Hairdryer. Hope this helped!
    - The Makeup Lady

$5 POPCORN (MOVIE REVIEWS)

Seen any movies lately that you wanna review? Send them to us. They can be rentals or ones you've seen recently at the theater.

Our rating system is as follows: 1- stinkbug (the movie stunk) 2- mosquito (annoying, u wanted to smack the actors) 3- house fly (tolerable) 4- super fly (it was pretty kewl) 5- Butta-fly (the kewlest)

The Truman Show (5-Butta-Fly)

This is one of the hottest tickets of the summer! Jim Carrey stars as Truman Burbank, a guy who's been a TV star since birth as the whole world has watched him grow up. Truman doesn't know this and just lives life as normal. He doesn't notice that his wife (played wonderfully by Laura Linney) is plugging products in their home, even though it's obvious! Truman is basically just a nice guy, trying to live a normal life.   One day, he starts noticing things are a little strange.  This is where the comedy begins! My only complaint is that the movie was a little long! Otherwise, it was well worth you $7 movie ticket!

The Horse Whisperer (5-Butta-Fly)

The Horse Whisperer is a beautiful film directed by Robert Redford. Redford also stars in the movie alongside Kirsten Scott Thomas and new-comer Scarlett Johansen. This movie is a tear-jerker and if you're a horse fan, some of the scenes might be disturbing. In fact, the accident scene is disturbing for almost anyone. I don't want to give away too much of the plot because it is a wonderful film. It is pretty serious, but there are a lot of light-hearted funny moments. I highly recommend this film.

DECORATING DIVA

Hi all you girlies out there! Welcome to the kewl world of decorating! Okay, so you may think that decorating is not your thing, or that you have no talent what so ever, but give it a chance! It's actually really fun, easy, & gives your room a personalized feel! Plus, you don't need gobs on ingenious creativity to pull it off! In this column, I'll share with ya' a few ideas on how to spruce up your space!

Try decorating your switchplate! You could use dried flowers, or even pressed flowers, and hot glue them on! Some stores, like "Lotions and Potions" carry pressed flower stickers! Stickers are another great idea! You could try using a really nice, LaRgE sticker & stick it on there, so that it fills up the whole plate, leaving no white spots! Just don't forget to cut out a hole in the middle for the switch! (But, you knew that!) Some more ideas could be using permanent marker, puff paints- you could even hot glue friendship bracelets you made onto it! The possibilities are endless! Just let your imagination take over!
Maybe you'd like to go for a less subtle, more dramatic approach. In this case, painting your walls a rad shade would be the answer! All you basically need is a paintbrush, paint, and someone who knows what they're doing! (You could always pay somebody, or have good ol' dad do the job!) Here are what certain colors can do for your room:

Blue:
The color of the ocean & sky, blue evokes feelings of peace & harmony. It's relaxing qualities can relieve stress & anxiety.

Purple:
This hue evokes images of glamour! Since purple is really a combination of red & blue, it combines feelings of excitement (from the red) with responsibility (from the blue).

Green:
Being the color of nature, green is equal to balance, growth, & peace. It has a calming effect.

Of course, if you go darker or lighter into those shades, they can create stronger feelings, such as Navy Blue would create a more responsible feeling.

Whatever color you choose, make sure you like it! If you don't want to be so dramatic, try off-white as an alternative. Or, you could keep the wall white, but sponge or paint on little designs all over! When it's all painted and totally neato, making things in your room match the color of the walls would make everything tie together more! To save money, you could make things to match, or you could reuse old things in your room- by repainting or altering the design!

I hope you liked some of those ideas, and I hope they are useful!
