QUIZ: HOW IS YOUR WORK ETHIC?

You have a big group project in History. You share the work:

A. Equally. You know everyone should do their part.

B. Let the smartest person do most of the work, after all they should use those brains!

C.  Work part of the time and let the others finish the project.

When you're babysitting, you usually:

A. Play with the kids a lot.

B. Talk on the phone the entire time.

C. Sit the kids in front of the TV for the duration of your stay.

How do you get most of your money?

A. By earning it!

B. By stealing it out of your mom's purse.

C. Mooching it off of your buds.

Your employer (or teacher if you don't have a job) would describe you as:

A. Hardworking

B. Fired (you can't hold a job or stay in school)

C. Lazy

When you're on a sports team, you always:

A. Try your hardest

B. Talk and sit around during practice.

C. Remain at the sidelines.

Scoring:

Mostly A's:  You have an awesome work ethic! Keep up your impressive skills. Even if you're not the best at something, you always get it everything you've got. You try to be fair and non-judgmental in whatever you do.

Mostly B's: Basically you're work ethic sucks. You try to use people and take the credit. If you want to take the credit, you should work for it. Try helping someone out instead of just using them.

Mostly C's: You have a fairly lazy work ethic. Get up and do something! We all procrastinate sometimes but you have to get up and work for what you want.

SPORTZ

Hey all you Butta-Fly Girlz out there. Today I have a short column becuz I was gone on vacation for quite awhile, and I didn't have mucho time to put together all your questions!

Q. Dear Sam,
I'd love to start an exercise program, but I have problems sticking to my plan. I just can't seem to get motivated!
~Lazy~

A. Dear Lazy,
Aloha! Seems to me that you need some motivation (obviously). I suggest getting a training partner. You guys can exercise together, and having someone with you will make it more interesting. If you prefer to exercise by yourself, think about that size 5 string bikini you've been longing to get into. Then, go for it! Hope I helped!

As summer comes to an end, a lot of summer bums out there are frantically looking for a fast way to lose weight before school starts. I have news. No matter how much dieting, exercise, or whatever you do, you are not going to be able to work it off before school starts. If you do get started on a weight loss plan now though, you will be feeling and looking better before school starts. Weight loss comes later.

Try these:
Eat right: Try carrots, apples, cheese and crackers, pretzels, lowfat popcorn, ricecakes, peanuts, and sandwiches. Believe it or not, sandwiches are good for you.

Exercise: I'm not talking stretching here. I mean sweat.

Keep your mind busy: Never let yourself get bored. Read a book or write a poem. Anything to get your butt off that couch!

Well, that's it for this issue. Until next time, Sam.

POPCORN (MOVIE REVIEWS)

Seen any movies lately that you wanna review? Send them to us. They can be rentals or ones you've seen recently at the theater.

Our rating system is as follows: 1- stinkbug (the movie stunk) 2- mosquito (annoying, u wanted to smack the actors) 3- house fly (tolerable) 4- super fly (it was pretty kewl) 5- Butta-fly (the kewlest)

Armageddon (5-Butta-Fly)

A truly awesome flick starring a couple of my fave actors- Ben Affleck and Liv Tyler. This is the ONLY action movie that has made me cry. Plot in a couple sentences: Earth is going to  blow up if it gets hit by this big asteroid. NASA sends a crew of oil drillers(Ben who plays AJ is one of them) to the asteroid to blow it up. I think this movie also has one of the sexiest scenes in it, involving Ben and Liv and some animal crackers. It is the cutest thing. I just melt!

Out of Sight (5-Butta-Fly)

I wanted to see this movie for so long. I learned one thing from this movie- I hate Jennifer Lopez. She's too pretty and perfect. George Clooney plays a charming bank robber who escapes from prison and tries to keep away from the police. The best performance in this movie was by Steve Zahn. He plays a super funny criminal who always wears sunglasses.  Michael Keaton and Albert Brooks have cameo roles in the film as well. It is filled with action, comedy, and romance. Don't delay any more, see it now!

Ever After (5-Butta-Fly)

I was a little leery of this film when I first went to see it because I often dislike period films. (That basically means when its people dressed like a long time ago) But this movie surprised me! Drew Barrymore was awesome in this Cinderella story. The plot is a amusing twist on the timeless fairy tale. The story is full of jokes and I was smiling and giggling the whole way through. If you're looking for a sweet romantic story that's not too soppy, then this is definitely a good one to see!

Halloween H20 (3-House Fly)

I love scary movies especially those creepy ones from the seventies so I was thrilled when I saw a new addition to one of the classics was coming out! This flick starring Jamie Lee Curtis was pretty scary at times. I know I jumped a couple of times. Jamie Lee Curtis plays a headmistress for a Northern California privates school who is trying to forget her past. If you've seen the first Halloween you'd understand why. Well the plot is pretty detailed and sort of hard to explain but she has a really hot son who is annoyed at her reappearing fear. The film also stars Michelle Williams (from Dawson's Creek). Anyway, if you're looking for a fair scare, then you gotta see H20.

DECORATING DIVA

Hello again! Welcome back! This issues Decorating Diva is going to talk about totally re-doing your entire room- within a budget.

*Mail Bag*

DeCoRgRl,

When I was in the third grade, my mom went all out and decorated my whole
room a dull pink. Everything in my room matches, the bedsheets, the comforter, the curtains, the rims of my desk, windowseat, and shelf, my blinds even! Now, I am in the sixth grade and I don't know how I can redecorate my room to a light denim color that matches the rims of my desk etc., within my budget of 300 bucks. Any Ideas? -Iris

Iris,

First of all, don't be worried about sending in questions! That's what makes a column real and interactive! Never hesitate to ask me a Q! Well, as far as wanting to change your entire room- start out small. Sprinkle denim colored things throughout your room, and keep adding denim colored things, little by little.
Here's some ideas:
• Use a light denim colored piece of fabric and cover the seat of your chair with it.
• Use the same fabric to cover picture frames, or even make your own,
by covering cardboard.
• Make a make-shift pillow: Take two denim colored bandanas and a quilt-batting pillow (it's a pre-made white little pillow you can find at any craft store) and put it between the two bandanas. Take colorful rubber bands, or elastic hair bands and tie the ends of the bandanas together at each corner! Tuck the sides in to make sure you can't see the pillow underneath and you have an instant pillow!
• Buy some denim lampshades

You can also make your bed the center of attention! Keep your eyes peeled for bedspread sales in the stores, and see if they have any in the denim color you want. I did this once- we got the whole bed set: Comforter, Pillows, Sheets, everything for just over $100! Then, the eye would be drawn to the bed, and when you placed denim colored objects throughout the room, the denim would be just as noticeable, or even more noticeable as the pink! You can also cover your window seat in denim fabric, just like the chair cushion. Just experiment, be creative, and look through decorating magazines to get inspiration! I hope I was helpful to our reader as well as anyone else who has had or has this prob! Have a nice day!

Unfortunately, that was the Decorating Diva's last column. Now I will be taking over the column. If you have any questions, comments, whatever. Send them here!

In the new computer orientated age, students carry disks everywhere. The only drawback to the folders is that some of them have little kiddish designs. But most of them are awesome! My favorite keychain was the Sushi Cat one.

Nifty Clipboards (5- Butta-Fly) These clipboards are great because they don't weight that much and they are still very sturdy. They have a cool looking durable clip to keep your papers in place. They also have a keychain hanging off the side. Plus these boards fit in your binder (they've got three rings). My favorite had a Felix the Cat design on it. Forties cartoons are super cool.

Nifty Paper (3-House Fly) The paper is made of really nice stuff and it looks cool but it's 10 1/2 by 8 inches which my teachers don't accept. This is not the standard size paper. The paper is better quality than most though. The Note taking paper was pretty cool though. It had two columns on it for topic and note taking. It organized the paper very well.

Nifty Binders (5- Butta Fly) Both of the binders were really cool. They have space savers in them (which is like a built-in pencil box. A little magnetic clip keeps all you stuff together. I really liked the two-hole binder because it was really good for taking notes. But most of my classes need three hole punched notebooks so I'll save the two hole one for doing report notes.

Nifty Paper Keepers(5-Butta Fly) I've never seen these things before. They are cute little clips that attach through your holes in binder paper to hold them together. It's like a staple without making little holes. The clips have little designs on them and they are super cute. You can also use them on your shoelaces, pencil toppers or for hairclips.

Nifty Math Pack and Calculator (5-Butta Fly) I really could have used this last year for geometry. The Math Pack includes a ruler, triangle, and protractor plus a really sturdy compass. Also on the side of the pack,are some helpful geometry hints. The little calculator is on a key chain and it is so cute. The only problem with it is that the buttons are so small that you can sometimes hit a wrong number. But the space it saves, it's definitely worth it.

GEE! THAT'S CATCHY (MUSIC COLUMN)

Send in your Music Reviews to AllisonGr

Radiohead- Ok Computer

Radiohead has been one of the most influential bands of this decade with their mellow alternative sound. This CD is truly amazing and it can bring me through a variety of moods. It's really good when I want to veg out and be mellow but it's also awesome to blast and rock out to while I'm driving. Everyone knows the song "Karma Police". They played it a lot on MTV and it became really popular. Every other song on the CD is just as if not more awesome than that song. This is a must have addition to you CD collection!

Punk-O-Rama 3

This various artist CD is kickin' with tracks from NOFX, Voodoo Glow Skulls, Rancid, Osker, Millenclin, Bad Religion, Pennywise, and many more. 25 tracks for under 4 bucks! This sampler CD from Epitaph records was such a good deal and the music on it totally rocks so to speak. Tons of well known punk and ska bands are on this CD sampling just some on the selection at Epitaph. Most of the songs on this CD are tracks on the bands own albums but it also includes two unreleased songs from NOFX and Pennywise. This CD is awesome.

Music Tip from Allison: Are you looking to expand your CD collection but don't have too much cash? Search the Used CD section often. New stuff comes in all the time and you can usually find something pretty good for way less than the normal price. When you bring your purchase to the counter, make sure there are no scratches on the CD. Another trick? Look for compilation CDs. Many record labels make samplers and sell them in the various artist section on the music store. Usually they cost six bucks or less and have music from the label's best bands. I recently purchased one from Asian Man Records that I just love for four bucks in the Punk/Ska section of the Wherehouse.
