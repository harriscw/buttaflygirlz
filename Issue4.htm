GUYS: HOW TO START THE NEW YEAR SURROUNDED WITH GUYS

The New Year is a perfect time to turn your guy troubles around. In this article we will have tips on how to snag you boy and hopefully keep him wrapped around your little finger.

Flirty Moves:

  1. Make eye contact with your crush and keep looking until you see him look back, then look down. And look back up again and if he's still looking, flash him your biggest smile.

  2. When you finally get up the nerve to talk to him, do more than ask yes or no questions. Like so the conversation goes somewhere. And when you get to an important point, gently touch his arm for a second. Don't make it seem obvious, but just long enough for him to realize it was there.

  3. When you're talking to him talk about an event that you want to go to. If he wants to go too, then maybe you can go together. Get it! It doesn't even have to be a date, it's just a way to get know each other better. Examples could be the movies, a party, or some food you've craving (like you know when you really want say a taco, and you say it, then someone else suddenly says they want a taco too. This is my definition of fate!)

Now, that you're going out:

  1. Don't embarrass him in front of his buds. He will never hear the end of it, so leave the cutesy love name for when you're alone.

  2. Communicate often. I know this sounds obvious but many couples don't do enough conversing and they drift apart. So talk a lot with each other! Especially if something about the relationship is bugging you. If doesn't know something is wrong, how's it going change.

HANSON ARTICLE BY LillyZBrat

I'm sure this has been in all sorts of newsletters before, but I wanted to see this in Butta-Fly Girlz because I know a lot of you probably have the same opinion but haven't had the time to Speak Out. I'm not a Hanson lover nor am I a Hanson hater. I listen to their music when I'm in the mood, but you couldn't say I really like them or that I don't. What I don't like, however, is the way this band is treated. When they sang at the World Series, they put all their effort and heart into the NATIONAL anthem...and what did the people do? Many people booed at them. They are just kids singing for their country but yet their HAIR makes them unpopular? They have to have some musical talent or they wouldn't have sung there, right? They did the very best job they could have done being their age and all, and yet other children as well as adults booed at them. The whole world has the wrong opinion about the group. Don't be hard on them. If you don't like them then just shut up. Keep it to yourself, because it's not going to get you or them anywhere. Hate is a strong word, so be a neutral person or even more to the dislike meter but don't hate them. They haven't done anything to you. You should respect their talent and live with it.

Dear Makeup Lady,
I have a best friend, and she is really pretty but she just doesn't try. She could be really pretty if she applied some makeup to her, she has really dark skin, and a beautiful complexion, but her hair is a mess. I try to tell her that she should blow-dry it but she doesn't listen, can you tell me one way me and my friend could maybe hint around to her that we can help her fix herself up? Thanks.
        -Anonymous

Dear Anonymous,
Have a slumber party with all your buds and do makeovers on each other. You should do makeup and hair! Not only will you have a blast but you will also help her with her hair. Do it and show her how to do it! Make sure to tell her how good it looks becuz if you don't reassure her, she'll just go back to her old way of doing her hair. Also you should make sure she likes how you did her hair before pressuring her to keep it that way. Don't put down her hairstyle, just make suggestions like "Your hair would look SOOO good, if you put it up" or something like that. Good luck with the makeovers.
        -The Makeup Lady

This issue we are going to suggest two makeup books that we love. The first is Kevin Aucoin's Making Faces. It has many really good tips on makeup and it even shows you how to pull off many classic styles such as Marilyn Monroe's Bombshell look, the Vamp look, the innocent look and many more. It has makeovers of regular people and stars like Drew Barrymore, Janet Jackson, Lisa Marie Presley, Vanessa Williams, and Gwyneth Paltrow. This book is very good at going step by step and I highly recommend it to any makeup lover. It also shows you how to correctly kohl line your eyes, which is good cuz I never really got it before!

The next book is Paula's Don't go to the Makeup Counter Without Me. It doesn't teach you any makeup tips, but rates almost all of the makeup out there. She also talks about many skin care products such Alpha Hydroxy, Accutane, Cellux C, and tells you what order to put your makeup on to make it flawless. I highly recommend this book is you are not sure if you want to buy a makeup product, because she has critiqued it and tells you if the product is worth buying or not.

SPORTZ: TAE KWON DO BY Yugin

The Sports everyone plays are like Baseball, Soccer (even though I play that also), and Basketball, but no one really knows that Martial Arts is a sport. I am in Tae Kwon Do, and it is great. The working out part is doing your form or having a Sparring session. Sparring is where you fight with another person, you have to have protective gear on and there are limits on where you can hit, but it gets you to learn self-defense and to build up your self-confidence while getting a work out. Another part of Tae Kwon Do I like is the Board Breaks. Everyone thinks they are hard but it's really easy if you know what you're doing. Now I am a Red belt, I have only 2 more belt's until I am a Black belt (I'm in ATA American Tae Kwon Do Association), and I can't wait. Everyone should try it out to see how it's like, because I did and now I don't regret it.
