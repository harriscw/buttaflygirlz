Dear Makeup Lady,
I normally wear my makeup pretty natural but for the holiday season, I'm going to be going to a lot of nighttime parties (some formal) and I want to make sure my makeup is not underdone. Have any ideas?
    -Normally Natural

Dear Normally Natural,
I usually wear pretty natural makeup during the day too, so I know just how you feel. You might want to just add a bit more color at night to have a more dramatic, striking look. Berry-stained lips look awesome during winter. You don't want to overkill it though because you're usually pretty natural. I would suggest applying the color and then blotting with a tissue. Add a layer of gloss for shine. Or wear a shear red/berry lip color. I loved Trucco's Fatal- a sorta sparkly red color. It reminds me of the glamour girls from the 40s and 50s. On the eyes you can either do a light shimmery thing or for low-key style go with basic smoky brown or rose. For all over shimmer try Maybelline's new Cosmic Edge line. It's a little dramatic but it looks pretty cool. I would be able to pull it off but for some people it would look awesome! Try the All Over Shimmer Powder for Face and Body. It looks really cool when you dust it on your neck and shoulders. Only use blush if you want to. It adds a wholesome rosy look to your face. Just put it on the apples of your checks. Also you can look really chic without changing much of your makeup style. Look at Gwenyth Paltrow for inspiration. She usually just adds a brighter lipstick to her evening attire. Or she opts for smoky eyes. It looks really good when you do up just one of your features (i.e. lips or eyes).
    - The Makeup Lady

Dear Makeup Lady,
Should I polish my toes even in the winter?
   -Tootsie

Dear Tootsie,
Go for it if you like your toes polished. I usually keep mine polished. Most nail polishes protect you nail but you should give them a break every couple of weeks. The worst thing for them is the nail polish remover but the remover is way better than picking at your nails. Picking the polish off damages your nail bed and makes it uneven and more susceptible to breakage. If you don't like the look of polish but want to keep your feet protected, use clear polish.
    - The Makeup Lady

POPCORN (MOVIE REVIEWS)

Seen any movies lately that you wanna review? Send them to us. They can be rentals or ones you've seen recently at the theater.

Our rating system is as follows: 1- stinkbug (the movie stunk) 2- mosquito (annoying, u wanted to smack the actors) 3- house fly (tolerable) 4- super fly (it was pretty kewl) 5- Butta-fly (the kewlest)

Next Stop Wonderland(5-Butta-Fly)

Wow! This indie flick is amazing. I didn't know much about it before I saw it but it was really cute, funny and romantic. It's basically about this girl whose longtime boyfriend just broke up with her. Her flamboyant mother wants to see her daughter happy so she writes her a personal ad. The scene where she is talking to her prospective dates is soooo funny! You just feel bad for her. After going through a whole bunch of failed dates, she meets this guy (that looks a lot like Antonio from Wings, in fact I lost a bet about this!) and he wants her to move to Brazil (or something) with him. The movie is super cute and I recommend it to all, especially people that like romantic comedies. But it's not disgustingly sweet like most of those movies, its more lifelike.

Pleasantville (5-Butta-Fly)

We've all heard of this flick. It got outstanding reviews and is worthy of all of them. The most interesting aspect of this movie was the whole black and white/color thing. This movies stars Reese Witherspoon, Tobey Maguire, William H. Macy, and Jeff Daniels. It starts out in the nineties where Reese and Tobey are twins who are in two completely different worlds. Reese is popular and outgoing; Tobey is more of the "dorky" type. He enjoys classic TV shows like "Pleasantville." Well, they get transported to Pleasantville (it takes to long to explain how) and they realize that the people are naive to many things, mainly sex. Reese starts a big scandal and makes a guy see colors for the first time. People start becoming color when they are "enlightened" from whatever they've been missing their whole life. The movie is filled with clever literary allusions and the court scene parallels the one in "To Kill A Mockingbird" (which is one of my favorite books). The movie is clever, funny and really cool watching things turn color while over things are black and white. I didn't do much justice to the plot in this review because it is very in-depth.

Video Pick:

North By Northwest(5-Butta-Fly)

Hitchcock is so cool. This is one of his best starring Carey Grant and Eva Marie Saint. It's not real scary but it's pretty suspenseful and always goes the opposite direction of what you think will happen. Carey Grant gets framed for murdering a UN guy and the movie follows him fleeing the police and trying to find the real killer. Saint plays a women that Grant falls for. She leads him into more danger but he still wants her. The end of the movie has an awesome fight scene on top of Mount Rushmore.

You should talk to him about it. You'll never understand where he is coming from if you don't ask.

-Al and Val

Hey~
I was wondering if you had any ideas on how I could Decorate my room. I have to share it w/My little sister and she doesn't like the same things I do. we decided to split the room using beaded curtains but I need ideas on what I can get for like under 200 bucks, I want to use blue and purple to decorate, but I don't know how to within 200 dollars! plus I only have like 2 feet in my room to work w/ cuz now it's smaller. do you have any ideas?
~Valerie

Here a some ideas:

Collage Cool

Want to add some style to your room for cheap? Then make a collage. It's super easy and it reflects your own awesome self. Go through old magazines and catalogs and cut out pictures of things you like. Be sure to cut out words and phrases too because they add meaning to your collage. I even made a collage once that was entirely sexist/non-feminist phrases. It was pretty powerful but not something I would want to hang in my room since it had a sort of gloomy feel to it. Well after you collected a bunch of pictures, get a piece of posterboard (you can use old calendar backs too) and layer your pictures on top of it how you want them arranged. Then tack them on using rubber cement or glue. I prefer rubber cement because it's way smoother. You can also do this with photos for a personalized decoration. I even framed one of my collages. It made it look like I bought it somewhere. If you want to do this buy a frame at a discount store and repaint it to match your room. Try acrylic paints. They come in a variety of colors (even metallics) and cost between $1-3 a bottle. One bottle should  be more than enough to paint most frames.

More Cheap Accessories for Your Room

You can get tons of cool, cheap decorations at discount stores. For cheap mood-enhancing candles try Target. Also look in the clearance section because lots of times items found here can inspire your creativity (without going out of your budget). I found a frame once at Target for $1.50 but I didn't like the color of it. I didn't feel like buying new paint so I decided to paint it with an old bottle of nail polish. It came out looking ultra-cute. I applied a "top coat" to seal the paint. Also try Cost Plus and Pier One Imports for unique ethnic accessories. You can find really cool silver Mexican mirrors at Cost Plus at reasonable prices. Urban Outfitters also carries some pretty awesome modern stuff. I bought a Chinese lantern there for $5. I probably could have gotten it even cheaper in Chinatown though.

Bargain Bed Stuff

A lot of times places like Target and Mervyns have little Bed in a Bag sets. They have a comforter, sheets, and pillow cases packed in a big bag. You can usually get them for between $20-30 on sale. Some of the designs are pretty good and others are really ugly. You just have to search for a cute one. Other times big stores (Macys, Mervyns, Target, Sears, etc.) have bed and bath sales. This is when you can get tons of great deals on lots of comforters and sheet sets. Sometimes Ross and Marshall's have cute bedding. You just have to check to see if you like what they have. If you find a basic solid color comforter that you like, you can easily make yourself some stylin' pillow cases. Just purchase some fabric at the store. Fold the fabric in half to the desired pillow case length. Then fold the top edges of the fabric 1/2 inch and sew a seam. Then turn the fabric inside out and stitch the remaining side. Leave the top of the pillow case unsewn so that you can slip your pillow inside.

FASHION STATEMENT

A new school year always brings new fashion. Here are some trends I 've noticed at school in Northern Cali:

Knee-High Boots- The kind that are tight and zip up the side. They make legs look slim too!
Dragons- Chinese influenced dragons are everywhere. Guys and girls both sport shirts and jackets with this awesome symbol.
Pedal Pushers- I personally hate this trend but many people are wearing them. I think they tend to look unflattering unless you're really skinny and have shapely legs.
Baseball Jerseys- I stole my little brother's old one. They're fun to wear, they remind you of when you were little. You can also purchase them from catalogs like Delia*s. Or buy one for cheap at a local sporting good store.
3/4 length sleeves- This sleeve length is perfect for fall. Not too cold, not too warm.
Grey- The sophisticated color of the season. Grey pants can be dressed up or down. I found a super-comfy pair at Old Navy for $34.
