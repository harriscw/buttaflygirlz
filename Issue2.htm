Mmmm! Recipes In honor of this season's makeup trend (spice colors), I will give you the recipe for my aunt's yummy ginger cookies.

Ginger Crinkles (makes 5 dozen)

Ingredients: 2/3 cup oil, 1 cup sugar, 1 egg, 4 Tablespoons molasses, 2 cups sifted flour, 2 teaspoons baking soda, 1/2 teaspoon salt, 1 teaspoon cinnamon, 1 teaspoon ginger, 1/4 c. granulated sugar for dripping

Preparations: Heat oven to 350º (moderate) Mix oil and sugar thoroughly. Add egg and beat well. Stir in molasses. Sift dry ingredients together and add to wet mixture. Drop teaspoonfuls of the dough into the dripping sugar and form into balls coated with sugar. Place on an ungreased cookie sheet, 3 inches apart. Bake for 15 minutes. Remove to wire rack. Cookies will flatten and crinkle.

Potato Latkes are the classic Hanukkah treat, delicious when topped with applesauce or sour cream.

Ingredients: 5 large potatoes, 1 large onion, 4 eggs, 1/4 matzo meal or flour, salt and pepper to taste, oil for frying, pinch of baking powder (optional)

Preparations: Grate potatoes and strain through colander. Grate onion. Add grated onion and eggs to potatoes, Mix well. Add matzo meal or flour and seasoning, Mix well. Heat oil in frying pan. Add mixture into oil, one tablespoon at a time until a round circle 3 1/2 inches in diameter forms. When golden brown, turn over and brown on other side.

I made one for Valerie as part of her birthday present this year, I included inside joke songs which made both of us laugh.

We got another idea for a cheap present from Seventeen magazine. A reader sent in a beauty tip to make lipstick kits. You take empty eye-shadow containers or pillboxes (the everyday of the week kind) and fill them up with mashed lipsticks. This is how you do it: You buy some cheap lipsticks (I recommend Wet and Wild cuz they only cost a buck) and put them in the fridge for a couple of hours. Then, you cut up the lipstick into chunks and mush them into the separate squares of the pillbox or compact. Then you use a lip brush to apply and mix the different shades. They are fun to make and mix and fairly cheap too!
