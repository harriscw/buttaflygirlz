QUIZ: ARE YOU VAIN?

As you're leaving for school, you pass a mirror, you:

A. Run by without even glancing

B. Check quickly to make sure your hair's looks OK

C. Re-apply your "face" for 5 minutes

Your bud looks really good in her new outfit, you think:

A.  "I never look at clothes"

B. "I'll complement her on the great outfit"

C. "I should borrow that outfit, if she looks that good, I'll look like a model!"

To you a mirror, is:

A. A piece of reflective glass

B. Useful after eating

C.  Essential for complementing yourself

To you a daring outfit you show:

A. Your ankles

B. Your shoulders and legs

C. Your stomach, back, thighs and chest

You get your school pics back, you:

A. Block it out of memory and shove them in a drawer

B. Shrug and hand them out to your friends

C. Order a poster-size version and hang it from your ceiling

Scoring:

Mostly A's: Be proud of your looks and who you are. You shouldn't be ashamed of yourself. Emphasize your good features. And don't see the mirror as your worst enemy!

Mostly B's: Way to go girlfriend! You have a good perception of yourself. You never seem so involved with your looks that you are unapproachable. Keep it up!

Mostly C's: Whoa chica! There's a difference between loving yourself and LOVING yourself. You gotta learn to keep them straight. There's nothing wrong with having self-confidence but tone it down a bit!

A VEGGIES VIEW BY Klaywomen

I've noticed that vegetarianism has recently become trendy. I think it's good that there is temporarily less meat-eating going on, but I don't think that this will last, as most trends fade. This bothers me because vegetarianism shouldn't be a trend, it's a lifestyle. I've been an ovo-lacto (meaning I eat eggs and milk products) vegetarianism for my whole life. It's been incredibly easy for me because I've never craved meat or even viewed it as food. But I can sympathize with meat-lovers' failed attempts at vegetarianism because I've tried many times to become a vegan (someone who eats NO animal products, even honey!) For me, just about everything I eat has some sort of animal product so it seems impossible to be a vegan. But there is a wide variety of foods to eat if you're just cutting meat out of your diet. If you're thinking of becoming a vegetarian, go for it! Even if it's only for a short side track from your regular life. But try to stick with it, Good Luck!

Send your makeup questions to AllisonGr or FlyGal102

Dear Makeup Lady,
What kind of mascara is kewl but doesn't make you look like a freak or outcast?
        -EcoGirl85

Dear EcoGirl85,
Some kewl mascara's to try might be blue or purple by Revlon StreetWear. Apply the mascara at the tips of the lashes for stand-out style.
        -The Makeup Lady

Dear Makeup Lady,
I have pretty oily skin. I use oil-free foundation but it sort of makes my face sweat or something. It makes my pimples all shiny. What can I do about this?
    -Savannah

Dear Savannah,
Maybe it is not a completely oil-free foundation. Check to see if it contains Silicon Oil. If so, try to find a foundation without any oils. If your foundation is already oil free, apply it lightly. Also you might want to finish by dusting your face with pressed powder to eliminate some of the excess oil.
    -The Makeup Lady

Dear Makeup Lady,
I can never get my eyeliner straight! I give up! I live with my dad and two bros so I can't get makeup tips from them! Help!
    -Eyeliner Clueless

Dear Eyeliner Clueless,
Don't use liquid liner, few can pull it off cuz it's difficult to master. I recommend using a self-sharpening pencil such as Maybelline Expert Eyes or Max Factor PenSilks. A cheaper option is Jane's One Liners. Also for starters, you may just want to apply it on the top lid. You can do this by pulling just outside the eyelid toward your ear to make the skin smooth in a single stroke movement. Remember less is better.
    -The Makeup Lady

Dear Makeup Lady,
I have slight acne. I mean it's not really really bad but it's really noticeable. I was wondering if you know of any good creams, scrubs, or washing gels, etc. to use for it? And also a really good looking cover-up to hide those ugly zits.
    - Don't know to do with my face

Dear Don't know what to do with my face,
I recommend Clean & Clear Foaming Facial Cleanser. It cleans skin while not irritating your zits. I don't recommend using scrubs since they may irritate your face more causing more redness. To get rid zits I suggest using either Neutrogena On-the-Spot Acne Treatment or Clean & Clear Invisible Blemish Treatment. The Neutrogena products takes longer but it causes less irritation. The Clean & Clear works quickly but sometimes leaves the face red. Another option is the Neutrogena Oil-Absorbing Acne Mask. This works really great on oily skin but if you have dry to normal skin, I would skip this product. A great cover-up is Jane Clueless to a Concealer. It provides matte coverage.
- The Makeup Lady

$5 POPCORN (MOVIE REVIEWS)

Seen any movies lately that you wanna review? Send them to us. They can be rentals or ones you've seen recently at the theater.

Our rating system is as follows: 1- stinkbug (the movie stunk) 2- mosquito (annoying, u wanted to smack the actors) 3- house fly (tolerable) 4- super fly (it was pretty kewl) 5- Butta-fly (the kewlest)

Good Will Hunting (5-Butta-fly)

This movie was SO good! The basic plot of this movie involves a really smart guy named Will(portrayed brilliantly by hottie Matt Damon) who works as a janitor at Harvard University. A math professor discovers his amazing ability and bails him out of jail after Will assaulted someone. In return Will agreed to study with the professor and to see a psychiatrist. After Will gets kicked out of many offices, the professor turns to his college buddy who happens to be a psychiatrist (played by Robin Williams). Some of Matt's psychological problems occur within his relationship with his girlfriend (Minnie Driver). Matt Damon and his best bud Ben Affleck co-write the screenplay for this inspiring movie. Affleck also stars as Damon's best friend. You have to see this movie!

As Good As It Gets (5-Butta-fly)

This movie was hilarious! Jack Nicholson was made for this role. He plays a person with and obsessive compulsive disorder who falls for a waitress (Helen Hunt) at the restaurant where he eats every morning. He is an extremely difficult man who is rude to everyone even the woman he likes. Greg Kinear plays his gay artist neighbor who Nicholson always interrogates. Cuba Gooding Jr. plays Kinear's manager who is always protecting him. Also Skeet Ulrich has a cameo appearance and you get to see his naked butt. There are tons of really funny jokes in this movie and if you're looking for a laugh I suggest you see it!

Wag the Dog (4-Super Fly)

This movie is so close to some current events happening right now that it is almost scary! The main plot involves a presidential sex scandal where the president has "his people"  (played by Anne Heche) hire a movie director (Dustin Hoffman) to make him a war. He does this so the attention from the sex scandal will diminish. This movie was really good at first but they dragged it on a little too long. The funniest part of the movie is a scene with Woody Harrelson, who plays the "hero of the war". This movie mocks the government and has many political jokes.

SPORTZ: CHEERLEADING by Lauren

Okay, okay. I know that when you saw the word Cheerleading you almost fell out of your seats laughing. You must be thinking, what does Cheerleading have to do with sports? Well I’m here as a cheerleader to let you know that it is very much so a sport. To start, lets dispel any old notions of what a typical cheerleader is. First, we are all not blonde. I happen to have brown tresses. Second, we are not all the most gorgeous and most popular girls in school. Third, we do have actual functioning brains. How is this proven? Because in order to stay on the Cheerleading squad, you must maintain a GPA of 2.5 or higher. And when you take Biology, Geometry, World History, and work, and are at practice 10 hours a week, it is not an easy feat to accomplish. But we do. What else do we accomplish? Lets talk about school spirit. At a rally, who performs at the beginning to get the student body psyched? The Cheerleaders. At a game, who performs cheers, trying to get the crowd involved, so that their team can hear the support of all it’s fans? The Cheerleaders. And on another note, who goes to senior citizens homes, day-cares, and elementary schools to make people a little happier? Yes, the Cheerleaders. And why do we do this? I do it because I love my school, I’m proud of it, and I’m willing to show it in anyway I can. We may not be star athletes, but we are athletic. I’m not saying that you should rush out and sign up for Cheerleading tryouts. I’m just saying that when you pass by the Cheerleaders, give them a little respect. They have the guts to show their pride, even if others don’t!

FASHION STATEMENT

Hey Chicas! I'm the new fashion statement writer! My name is Helen, I live in Cali, and I LOVE to shop!! If you have any comments send them to XOCuTeXO!

Must Haves for '98!
So what fashion must haves are out there? Check this out!

The first item of clothing you need is the classic dark denim jeans. They go with absolutely anything and are very classic and chic. Dress 'em up with a black tank top and fur trimmed jacket or dress 'em down in a plain white tee.

Skinny pants. They're black, tight and almost polyester. The newest rage, this fashion is fabulous! Very chic, yet very down to earth!

Chinoisiere. Am I talking in a foreign language? Not exactly. Chinoisiere is oriental prints found in skirts, dresses, pants and tee shirts. Very glamorous, this look is very versible. Contempo Casuals and Clothestime offer great oriental prints, but if you're into designer clothing, Prada is the best!

Platforms. Everyone says stilettos are in, but girls, go for your instincts. You can get platforms anywhere too! Try out the velvet platform slides for a glam yet comfy look. Platform sneakers are also very kewl!

Sweatshirts. The look for '98: I'm predicting 80's style, off-the-shoulder sweatshirts. They're comfy and cozy! Perfect for winter.

Gap White Tee. So like, it doesn't have to be Gap, but Gap is the best. Anyways, you need a white tee like year round so it's good to have one that is better quality. Regular white tees are perfect for wearing underneath a tank, and they match anything!
