HOLY MOSELEY!

March 1, 1998 - Tiburon, California

Today I went to a parade honoring Olympic Gold Medallist Jonny Moseley. Jonny was the first U.S. medal winner in this year's Winter Olympics in Nagano, Japan! His flawless performance on the moguls included his trademark helicopter mute grab. Since then, he's had non-stop interviews and public appearances. His performance on David Letterman earned him the nickname Jonny "Big Air" Moseley! Today's parade was a huge deal in his hometown of Tiburon, California located just north of San Francisco. Between five and ten thousand fans showed up to greet their local hero!

Jonny Moseley was born in San Juan, Puerto Rico on August 27, 1975 (which makes him 22 years old). He learned how to ski when he was 4 at Squaw Valley, California (home of the 1960 Winter Games). Squaw is still Jonny's favorite ski resort. "It's always been my favorite, and I've skied plenty of places. Second favorite is Blackcomb/Whistler; it's year-round, and has a killer town."

When Jonny was younger, his supportive family, now called Team Moseley, would drive to Tahoe every weekend after the kids got home from school. Jonny's two older brothers, Jeff and Rick, are also skiers. Jonny's recent success was no doubt assisted by his dedicated family. Jonny obviously cares a lot for his family too since he had to stay away from them during the Olympics in order to stay focused. Jonny showed his affection for mother during their absence by saying, "I think my mom had a hard time with it."  Other members of Team Moseley include his friends, one of which said, "He went huge. He's a stud. I always knew he was a stud."

Jonny wasn't joking around when he commented on the David Letterman show that winning the gold medal took "A lot of hard work." He'd been working towards the goal for four years, training especially hard during the six months previous to the Olympics. When asked about his ski preference he said, "I ski K2 skis, solely, and I always will. The Power Mamba, 195 cm."  Jonny's trademark move is his helicopter mute grab (what's a mute grab?).  "You can see the crowd, that's the greatest part of it," Jonny says of the jump.

Jonny likes other stuff besides skiing too! Some of his favorite things include golfing, sailing, wakeboarding and water-skiing although he doesn't have much time to practice them anymore. On his music preference he commented, "Music's an important part of my life. There's a pretty big spread on what I listen to: NWA to Bad Religion, I like rap, fast stuff and almost anything in between." His takes great pride in his 1964 black Pontiac Bonneville covertible which Jonny is restoring. "I'm not very good but I grew up fixing things."
