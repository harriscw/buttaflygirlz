Dear Makeup Lady,
Help! I have combination skin and I never know where to put the right products. Any tips?
    -ComboClueless

Dear ComboClueless,
I suggest you use a mild cleanser cause it's gentle on zits and doesn't dehydrate your dry parts.  Use a moisturizer on the dry patches of your skin so they don't start to get scaly. I suggest Physician's Formula Vital Defense Moisture Cream with SPF 25. It keeps your skin soft and smooth but doesn't clog up your pores. It also has an added bonus of SPF cuz it's important to make sure your face is protected.
    - The Makeup Lady

Dear Makeup Lady,
Hi! What would be the best color of hair mascara to wear if you had dark blonde hair?
    - Lora

Dear Lora,
It depends on what kind of look you're going for. If you want natural looking highlights, then choose a platinum or golden blonde color. I use Revlon Street Wear's Blondie. If you want to be a little more daring and funky, try some copper or auburn highlights. These will brighten your hair's color and add a touch of red.
    -The Makeup Lady

Dear Al and Val,
Hi! I was wondering how to tell my friends that they are leaving me out of everything they do together because I am going out with a guy that they don't like. What should I do?
        - Sara

Dear Sara,
First you have to decide whether they are your true friends or not. True buds would not give you an ultimatum. You should also talk to them about why they don't like your boyfriend. Also, I know this sounds motherly, but remember good friends are more important than boyfriends. At this time in your life, boyfriends will come and go, it's your friends that help you deal threw good and bad times. What's really important is that you tell your friends that you are upset cuz they are leaving you out. If they still ignore you, it's time to say adios!
         -Al and Val

SPORTZ: GUYWATCHING

Okay, so most people don't consider this an actual sport, but girls you've gotta admit that sometimes it's hard not to break a sweat when a certain hottie walks by. The strategies of this sport are quite simple and an advanced guywatcher will be able to spy on the tasty treat without them even knowing, unless you want them to! Okay, first factor: location. Location is VERY important!! You are probably not going to find many studly subjects at the makeup counter in the department store or the nail salon, so you gotta think of someplace where many 'a fellows walks by. I suggest for beginners, the mall or the beach! Some other good options are the skate park, surf shop and the music store. Another important thing to remember is to station yourself so you can see the center of the action. This is so you will be able to watch an ideal amount of guys! I suggest bringing a friend with you so that you can look without being so obvious. Practice, practice, practice! The more you practice, the better you'll get,  not to mention, you'll probably get girl-watched by some hunks on a mission too!
