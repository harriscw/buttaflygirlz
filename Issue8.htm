THE MAKEUP COUNTER

Send your makeup questions to AllisonGr or FlyGal102

Dear Makeup Lady,
How do you help get rid of, or prevent hair breakage?
    -Adidas1098

Dear Adidas1098,
The things that usually cause hair breakage all occur during the process of drying your hair. When your hair is wet, don't brush it! It causes major hair breakage. Instead try using a wide-toothed comb and some leave-in conditioner for added protection. When blow-drying or curling your hair, the heat can also cause breakage. Try using Neutrogena HeatSafe to protect your hair from heat damage.
    - The Makeup Lady

Dear Makeup Lady,
I have reddish/brown hair and brown eyes! What are the best colors for me?
    -Lilac

Dear Lilac,
It sounds like you have warm coloring. Most cosmetic company's list their colors as either cool, warm, or neutral. You should try the warm and/or neutral colors. Neutral colors look good on almost everybody. Warm colors include browns, moss greens, warm blues, and rosy colors. Cool coloring has some vibrant and some pastel colors. Some kewl colors for spring are in Cover Girl's new Seashell line. Check them out and look for the label about warm/cool/neutral!
    -The Makeup Lady

$5 POPCORN (MOVIE REVIEWS)

Seen any movies lately that you wanna review? Send them to us. They can be rentals or ones you've seen recently at the theater.

Our rating system is as follows: 1- stinkbug (the movie stunk) 2- mosquito (annoying, u wanted to smack the actors) 3- house fly (tolerable) 4- super fly (it was pretty kewl) 5- Butta-fly (the kewlest)

The Full Monty (4-Super Fly)

This movie was hilarious! I never would have thought that I would enjoy a movie about old male strippers but it was so entertaining! The soundtrack to the movie was so perfect with tons of oldies that could be related to stripping. The basic plot is about a group of unemployed middle-aged men who decide to make some money by doing a one-time only strip show. The movie is clean though, as the only nudity is a butt. My favorite scene is when they are all standing in line at the unemployment office and they start to dance. If you're in the mood for a wacky flick, rent it today!

Hush (5-Butta-Fly)

I really liked this movie. It was full of suspense. It's about a wicked old woman, Martha, who deceived and lied to get her things her way. She is jealous of her daughter-in-law, Helen, because she seems to keep her son, Jackson, away from her, though that is not her intention. The only thing Martha can do to get her son back is kill Helen, after Helen gives birth to her baby boy, of course. (Martha wants a grandson.) Gwyneth Patrow is Helen, and Jessica Lange is Martha.(Review by JNH485)

The Man in the Iron Mask (4- Super Fly)

This review was compiled of two reviews sent in by BUBBLEPOP3 and TangyAqua) The movie was very good although it was a little graphic. It was almost better than Titanic but not quite. There is lots of great action and some emotional scenes too. It's definitely a must see!

Grease (5- Butta-Fly)

I just saw the movie GREASE in theaters with my friends. I know most people have seen it at home a thousand times, and I have to, but I have to say, it was SO AWESOME on screen! It was like the only happy movie I've seen on the movie screen , I mean with Titanic, Good Will, Contact, As good as it gets, . . . . .etc . . . . . It's nice to see such a happy movie for a change. It's awesome fun with an audience, 2! All of these ladies in their 50s and 60s dress up in the poodle skirts and scarves in their hair, with 50 pounds of makeup on, and the letterman jackets, and the tight black tops, it's just so funny. They also have their kids all decked up for the 50's! It was totally fun, though. During the songs we and the whole theater danced with the songs, clapped to them, and had such fun! Also, we clapped at the end, which is rare. It was awesome. We are going to see it again next week! I recommend going to your theater as soon as you can, it's great fun! (Reviewed by Michelle)

FASHION STATEMENT

Spring Fashion!!!

Over here in Cali, the weather is finally heating up! YaY!! So why not prepare yourself for the warm weather with some hot new duds?

Where should I go for new threads?

Sooo simple...The Gap is slowly getting some new merchandise, and you can stock up on your basics there. If you're not into shelling out the big bucks, head over to a local Factory Outlet. Most communities have 'em! Otherwise...Check out your fave stores, like Contempo, for some new tanks and shorts. Department Stores are already stocking up on tanks and tees, so don't dismiss them!

What is *in* for Spring?

Definitely check out your local Pacific Sunwear (if they have girly clothes) for some excellent trends! At my school, board shorts are already making a comeback. Khakis are almost a must-have for the pre-Spring wardrobe, along with capri pants and fishermen hats!

What about accessories?

Silver hoops are cute for Spring, along with pinkie rings and toe rings. But in Spring, it's the clothes that set the mood. So accessories are not always a necessary!
